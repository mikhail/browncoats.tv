Prototype thesis

Don’t let “too soon” happen again. Don’t use a landlord.

Firefly has a fanbase that has already proven long-term loyalty. A self-hosted official home would let the team speak to that audience directly.

Why self-host

Use platforms for reach. Keep the official home under Firefly’s control.

Platform distribution can reach viewers, but it often keeps the customer relationship, the merchandising path, the watch data, and the programming surface. A direct fan hub keeps Firefly’s front door under Firefly’s control.

📊

First-party fan relationship

Announcements, accounts, events, merch, and community can belong to the official team.

▶

Programming control

Premieres, watch parties, aftershows, and creator chats can be scheduled around fan rituals.

⚖

Stronger negotiating position

An owned destination gives the team more leverage before any broader streaming, licensing, or distribution deal.

The emotional pitch

Make it feel like coming home.

The point is not to run an unofficial Firefly service. The point is to show how warm, focused, and alive an official Browncoat home could feel.

Platform economics

Use platforms for reach. Preserve the first-party fan relationship.

Platforms can help Firefly reach people. They should not be the only place the official fan relationship lives.

YouTube

Huge reach, but the algorithm owns discovery and the audience relationship.

Amazon Prime Video Direct

Massive distribution, but selected/licensed at Amazon’s discretion with limited audience ownership.

Netflix

Premium exposure if selected, but not a self-serve official fan home.

Vimeo OTT / white-label

A possible white-label route for running a branded official hub.

Precedent: Dropout

Direct-to-fan for creative freedom and reliable audience economics.

Dropout shows why a dedicated fanbase may be better served by a direct home: clearer economics, stronger community, and less dependence on platform priorities.

Dropout’s context differs from Firefly’s. This is a directional precedent, not a 1:1 comparison.
